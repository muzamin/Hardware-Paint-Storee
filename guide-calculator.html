2. How to Measure Complex Architectural Spaces

01

Measuring Rectangular Rooms

Measure the perimeter of the room (Length + Width × 2) and multiply by the ceiling height. Measure standard door frames (typically 2 m² or 21 sq ft) and windows (typically 1.5 m² or 16 sq ft) and deduct these openings from the gross surface.

02

Calculating Vaulted & Gabled Ceilings

For triangular cathedral gables, measure the wall width at the base and the vertical height from the top of the flat wall to the peak of the gable. Multiply Base × Height and divide by 2. Add this area to the standard wall calculations.

03

Estimating Baseboard & Crown Moldings

Take the room perimeter in linear meters or feet. For baseboards, subtract door opening widths. Add a 10% cutting waste factor for 45-degree miter and scarf joints.
