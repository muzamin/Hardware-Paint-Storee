OPTICAL PHYSICS

1. The Architectural Paint Sheen Reflectance Spectrum

Sheen describes the specular gloss percentage reflected from a surface measured at a 60-degree angle. Higher sheen corresponds to a tighter, less porous resin binder—increasing washability while emphasizing drywall framing imperfections.

2% Gloss

Dead Flat Silicate

Completely diffuse light reflection. Disguises uneven drywall tape joints, plaster repairs, and ceiling flaws under harsh raking sunlight.

Best For: Master ceilings, formal living rooms, acoustic media suites.
7% Gloss

Matte Velvet Emulsion

Chalky tactile appearance with cross-linked ceramic micro-spheres. Can be wiped clean with a damp microfiber cloth without burnishing shiny marks.

Best For: Family living areas, bedrooms, home offices, executive suites.
18% Gloss

Eggshell Satin Shield

Light-catching, tight polymer skin. Repels moisture, grease, and condensation while maintaining a refined architectural appearance.

Best For: Kitchens, laundry rooms, bathrooms, main entry halls.
35% Gloss

Semi-Gloss Enamel

High-tensile scrub barrier. Resists scuffs from shoes, vacuum cleaner bumps, and acidic finger oils.

Best For: Interior doors, window casings, baseboards, custom cabinetry.

SUBSTRATE COMPATIBILITY

3. Substrate Material Finishing Considerations

Architectural Millwork & Timber

Hardwoods expand with humidity shifts. Use vegetable-alkyd paints or penetrating hard-wax oils that breathe with the wood fibers, preventing edge checks and peeling.

Primer:Shellac Stain-Block
Flexibility:High Elongation

Lime Plaster & Modern Drywall

Lime-plaster surfaces require breathable paints (Sd < 0.05m) to avoid trapping moisture inside masonry walls. Drywall requires non-penetrating acrylic bonding primer.

Permeability:Sd < 0.02m (Vapor Open)
Sheen:Dead Flat / Velvet

Architectural Structural Metals

Ferrous metals require rust-inhibitive zinc phosphate primers. Non-ferrous copper alloys should remain unsealed to develop organic natural tarnish over decades.

Salt Spray:ASTM B117 > 500 hrs
Coating:PVD / Unlacquered
