Store Inquiries & Visiting Policies

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding store visits, sample checkouts, custom paint color matching, and contractor accounts.

Our store is located at 450 Foundry & Artisan Blvd, District 7. We provide dedicated on-site customer parking stalls directly in front of the main showroom entrance, as well as covered parking with complimentary validation. Trade contractors have direct access to our covered drive-through loading bay at Gate 2.

No appointment is needed. Our showroom galleries are open to homeowners, architects, designers, and builders Monday through Saturday during regular store hours. However, if you are bringing comprehensive architectural door schedules or full-house paint plans, booking a private consultation ensures a dedicated specialist is reserved exclusively for your project.

Yes. Our in-store Color Studio is equipped with computerized spectrophotometers capable of reading opaque substrates including fabrics, tiles, stones, wood stains, and competitor paint chips. We formulate an exact mineral tint match on-site and can dispense 250ml sample test pots within 15 minutes.

Yes. We offer a complimentary sample checkout service for accredited designers, architects, and active homeowners. You can take home full-size A4 paint drawdowns, finish patina blocks, and working lever handles to evaluate them under your actual project lighting conditions.

Licensed contractors, painters, and tradespeople can register at our Trade Counter or submit credentials online through our contact form. Trade accounts receive tiered discounts (15% to 25% off standard rates), 30-day net invoicing, dedicated account management, and priority phone-in order staging.

Yes. We provide same-day will-call store pickup. Orders called in or submitted online are staged in our express collection lockers or at the contractor loading dock within 2 hours. We also provide local job-site courier delivery for heavy pallets and bulk orders.
